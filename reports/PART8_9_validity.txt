PART 8. The E-DAIC half of the conflict construction, as px_build.py actually does it
source: /Users/chaitanyaparwatkar/Desktop/af2_run/speech-health/scripts/audiollm/px_build.py (291 lines)
==============================================================================================================
The E-DAIC half takes one input feature set and one target. The input features are the raw words of each
segment, turned into text features by TfidfVectorizer(ngram_range=(1, 2), min_df=2, sublinear_tf=True) at
line 145, and in parallel four lexicon scalars val, nsent, npos, nneg produced at lines 137-139 by
LX.score on the same text; nothing acoustic enters the rule. The target label is the speaker-level
Depression_label merged from detailed_lables.csv at line 67 and carried onto every segment of that speaker
as S["y"] at line 127, with Depression_severity carried alongside as sev. The fold scheme is
StratifiedGroupKFold(5, shuffle=True, random_state=0) split on groups=pid at line 143, with an explicit
speaker-leak assertion at line 144, and each fold's held-out predict_proba is written back into the
out-of-fold vector oof at line 147, so every segment is scored by a model that never saw that speaker.
Each segment is scored twice, therefore: once by the lexicon into (val, npos, nneg) and once by the
out-of-fold TF-IDF logistic regression into oof in [0,1]. The eligibility gate at line 154 keeps only
segments with speech_s >= 5, nw >= 25 and span >= 8. The exact rule that marks a segment conflict is
lines 159-162: POSW = (val >= 5) & (npos >= 3) & (nneg == 0); NEGW = (val <= -4) & (nneg >= 2) & (npos == 0);
CONF = ((y == 1) & (sev >= 15) & POSW) | ((y == 0) & (sev <= 4) & NEGW), i.e. a segment is conflict when a
PHQ-8-depressed speaker with severity at least 15 produces strongly positive lexicon words, or a control
speaker with severity at most 4 produces strongly negative lexicon words, and the mirrored pairing
AGR = ((y == 1) & (sev >= 15) & NEGW) | ((y == 0) & (sev <= 4) & POSW) supplies the agreement partner,
matched on span within the same speaker at lines 183-190 with a looser fallback POSW2/NEGW2 at lines 164-166.

  and nneg (px_build.py line 12 `import dq_lex as LX`, resolved from sys.path.insert of
  /scratch1/parwatka/pd_probing/code_clean at line 11; scored at lines 137-139; used at lines 159-162).
  dq_lex is not on this Mac and not on the G-Drive (DISCREPANCIES.md D13). The manifest carries only its
  OUTPUTS per segment, not the lexicon, so no text can be rescored under the identical rule. Only the
  TF-IDF half, lines 141-149,, fully specified by public sklearn calls , is reproducible. Everything in
  PART 9 below is therefore a NAMED, PUBLIC substitute measured AGAINST the shipped dq_lex outputs, never
  a silent stand-in for them.
