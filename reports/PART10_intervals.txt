METHOD
  AUC is the Mann-Whitney rank statistic, average ranks for ties:
    r = pd.Series(s).rank().values ; n1=(y==1).sum() ; n0=(y==0).sum()
    auc = (r[y==1].sum() - n1*(n1+1)/2) / (n1*n0)
  Bootstrap: 2000 draws, numpy.random.default_rng(0) reseeded per cell, SPEAKERS resampled with
  replacement (all clips of a drawn speaker come along), percentile interval at 2.5 and 97.5.
  sklearn and scipy are not installed on this Mac, so nothing here uses them.
  10b draws the speaker list ONCE per replicate and recomputes both arms inside that same draw,
  so conflict and agreement move together and the delta interval is paired, not two independent CIs.
  A replicate where either arm loses a class is dropped and counted (column 'degenerate draws').
